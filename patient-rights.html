Patient Rights & Responsibilities

SafeCare Clinical Governance

Our commitment is to provide respectful, evidence-based care while protecting your dignity and privacy.

Your Rights as a Patient

  • Right to Respect: To receive care without discrimination based on race, religion, gender, or social status.
  • Right to Information: To be fully informed about your diagnosis, treatment plan, and possible clinical outcomes.
  • Right to Privacy: To have your medical records and consultations kept strictly confidential under the Data Protection Act.
  • Right to Consent: To accept or refuse proposed treatments and be informed of the medical consequences of refusal.
  • Right to Feedback: To voice complaints or compliments without fear of it affecting your quality of care.

Your Responsibilities

  • Accurate Information: Provide complete and accurate details about your medical history, past illnesses, and medications.
  • Compliance: Follow the agreed-upon treatment plan and instructions given by our clinical staff.
  • Respect: Treat facility staff, structure, and other patients with courtesy and respect.
  • Financial Obligations: Fulfil financial obligations for the healthcare services received promptly (Cash, M-Pesa, or valid Insurance/SHA).
