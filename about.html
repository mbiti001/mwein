Who We Are

Mwein Medical Services is a licensed private healthcare facility dedicated to providing comprehensive, quality care to our community with professionalism and compassion.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, quality healthcare services with compassion and professionalism, supporting the health and wellbeing of every patient we serve.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted healthcare provider in Busia County, known for quality, innovation, and measurable improvements in community health outcomes.

Our Values

Integrity, patient-centered care, professional excellence, and a deep commitment to community health guide every decision we make.

Why Choose Mwein?

We combine professional expertise with genuine care for every patient.

Professional Team

Trained and qualified healthcare professionals committed to delivering excellent patient care using the latest evidence-based practices.

24/7 Availability

We are open around the clock, 365 days a year. Emergencies don't keep business hours — and neither do we.

Quality Assurance

SafeCare-compliant standards with regular audits, infection prevention protocols, and patient safety as our highest priority.

Patient-Centered Care

Every care decision is made with the patient's wellbeing and dignity at the center. We listen, we respect, we care.

SHA & Insurance Accepted

We are a registered SHA network provider, with multiple insurance schemes accepted to reduce out-of-pocket costs for patients.

Accessible Location

Conveniently located in Mungatsi, Busia County — serving the local community and surrounding areas with ease of access.
