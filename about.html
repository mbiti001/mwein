Who We Are

Mwein Medical Services is a licensed private healthcare facility located in Mungatsi, Busia County, Kenya. We are committed to providing comprehensive, quality healthcare services to our community with a focus on patient satisfaction, professional excellence, and accessible healthcare for all.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, quality healthcare services with compassion and professionalism, supporting the health and wellbeing of our community.

Our Vision

To be a trusted healthcare provider known for quality, innovation, and commitment to improving health outcomes in our community.

Our Values

We are guided by principles of integrity, patient care, professional excellence, and commitment to community health and wellbeing.
