Practical information about visiting Mwein Medical Services in Mungatsi, Busia County.

Where is the clinic, and when are you open?

We are in Mungatsi, Busia County, and open 24 hours every day. Get directions to the clinic.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome; no appointment or advance message is required. An online enquiry sends a message and does not book an appointment. Ask our team about session times for scheduled services.

Can I use my SHA cover?

We accept SHA PHC and Public Officers cover, including TSC and Usalama. Bring your membership details so our team can confirm eligibility and covered services. Any charges outside your confirmed cover will be explained. Read about SHA cover.

When are antenatal care, ultrasound and vaccinations available?

Antenatal care and ultrasound are available every day. Vaccinations are on Mondays. Ask our team about session times and vaccine availability before a planned visit. Explore maternity and child health services.

Do you offer maternity and delivery care?

Our services include antenatal care, labour monitoring, normal deliveries, postnatal care and newborn care. Our team assesses your needs and arranges referral when further care is required. Read about maternity care.

What should I bring for SHA or a medicine refill?

For SHA, bring your membership details. For a medicine refill, bring your prescription or current medicine details. Our team will guide you through your visit. See the visit checklist.

How can I pay for my care?

Cash, M-Pesa and cheque are accepted for direct payments or charges outside confirmed cover or agreed care arrangements. Ask our team for payment or payee details. See payment options.

What should I do if I need urgent care?

Visit the clinic directly. Do not wait for a website, email or WhatsApp response. We provide emergency assessment and stabilisation within the clinic’s capacity, with referral when further care is needed. Find the clinic.

How can I ask a non-urgent question?

Use our enquiry form first, or email info@mweinmedical.co.ke. If you cannot use the form or need to follow up, send a WhatsApp message. The WhatsApp number is message-only; a dedicated call line is not yet available.

Still have a question?

Send a non-urgent enquiry to our team. An enquiry does not book an appointment.
