Laboratory and ultrasound

Laboratory testing

Ask about this service →

Tests to support diagnosis and follow-up, including malaria, blood sugar, blood count, urine and stool tests, HIV testing and antenatal profiles.

Ultrasound

Ask about this service →

Point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) for pregnancy monitoring and other clinical assessments.

More laboratory tests

View the test list

Salmonella tests, haemoglobin checks, antistreptolysin O (ASOT), hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg), H. pylori tests, peripheral blood film and syphilis testing (VDRL). Our team will advise which test is needed and confirm availability.

Urgent care and minor procedures

Emergency assessment and referral

Initial assessment and stabilisation for emergencies, including trauma and urgent gynaecological concerns. Care is provided within our facility’s capacity, with referral when further care is needed.

Minor procedures

Ask about this service →

Stitching, foreign body removal, male circumcision, and plaster of Paris application and removal. Our team assesses whether a procedure is appropriate at the clinic.
