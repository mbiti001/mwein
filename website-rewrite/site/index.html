A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you head over.

Still have a question?
Send a non-urgent enquiry

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. An online enquiry sends a message; it does not book an appointment. Ask our team about session times for scheduled services.

Can I use my SHA cover?

We accept SHA PHC and Public Officers cover, including TSC and Usalama. Bring your membership details so the team can confirm eligibility and covered services. Read about SHA cover.
