WEBSITE PRIVACY

Your information.

This public website

This website provides facility and contact information. It does not provide access to patient records. Our enquiry form collects your name, contact details, request type and message so our team can respond. Please do not include medical records, identification numbers or payment details. Hosting systems may record routine technical information such as IP addresses and page requests.

Contacting us

Start with our website enquiry form. Email and WhatsApp links open the corresponding service. The published number is for WhatsApp messages only; a dedicated call line is not yet available. Share only the information needed for your enquiry. Ask our team for a suitable, secure channel before sending medical records or other sensitive documents.

Cookies, storage and statistics

Website enquiries are stored on our hosting account and are accessible to authorised website administrators after sign-in. Contact administration to request correction or deletion of a message. Website enquiries are separate from clinical records.

The cookie panel offers Accept all, Reject optional and Necessary only. Reject optional and Necessary only both disable optional analytics; necessary security and preference storage remain available. You can reopen Cookie settings in the footer at any time. Optional analytics starts only after you choose Accept all. If you allow optional visitor analytics, first-party cookies recognise your browser for up to 90 days and group activity into sessions ending after 30 minutes of inactivity. We store keyed identifiers, first and last visit times, session page counts, referring domain only, broad device/browser type and approximate country. Your network address is looked up locally using DB-IP Lite; it is not stored in analytics or sent to a geolocation service. Country estimates can be inaccurate, especially with VPNs and mobile networks. We do not use GPS or link analytics to enquiry details. You can turn off optional analytics in Cookie settings; this removes the browser cookies. Inactive visitor/session records are removed after 90 days; aggregate page counts remain. Your choice is remembered locally for 90 days. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable tracking. Short-lived keyed network identifiers help limit automated submissions; hosting access logs are separate. Administrators use a secure session cookie to sign in. After admin sign-in, a separate signed cookie excludes that browser from visitor and page-view statistics for one year after its latest admin use, including after sign-out. This exclusion cookie does not grant account access. Previously recorded traffic totals may be archived separately when a new reporting period starts. IP Geolocation by DB-IP (CC BY 4.0).

Public comments and visitor reviews

If you submit a blog comment or clinic review, we store your chosen display name, message, submission time and (for reviews) your star rating, with your publication consent. Use a nickname and do not include medical details, identification numbers, phone numbers or information about other people. Submissions wait for moderation and are visible to authorised website administrators. Approved submissions display your chosen name, date, message and rating publicly. Moderation checks privacy, relevance, spam and abuse, not whether a rating is positive. Reviews are self-reported and are not matched to patient records. The average includes published ratings only.

We store moderation decisions and use short-lived keyed network identifiers to limit spam; raw network addresses are not stored in the feedback table. Signed form tokens prevent duplicate submissions. Use our contact form to request correction or removal, identifying the submission and approximate date. Choose Official correspondence. Feedback is separate from clinical records and is not a monitored emergency or medical-advice channel.

Other services

Google Fonts supplies the website’s typefaces and may receive routine connection information such as your IP address. Google Maps, WhatsApp and the separate EMR operate outside this public website. Their own access controls and applicable privacy arrangements govern your use of those services. This notice does not replace the facility’s procedures for protecting patient records.

Questions or requests

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form and choose Official correspondence.
