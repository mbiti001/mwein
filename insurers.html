About Our Facility

Mwein Medical Services is a private community healthcare facility located in Mungatsi, Busia County, Kenya. We are dedicated to providing accessible, professional, and compassionate healthcare services to our community.

Our facility is equipped to provide outpatient medical care, diagnostic laboratory services, ultrasound imaging, pharmacy services, antenatal care, immunization services, and management of chronic diseases. We operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to ensure healthcare access whenever patients need it.

How We Operate

24/7 Availability

Open round the clock, 7 days a week, including holidays and weekends.

Patient-Initiated Services

Walk-in patients and pre-booked appointments are both welcome. Call or use WhatsApp to schedule.

Appointments can be made at a time convenient for you. For medical emergencies, visit our clinic immediately—we are open 24 hours, 7 days a week.

Insurance & Payment Methods

SHA (Social Health Authority)

We are registered with and accept SHA for eligible services. SHA members receive covered benefits as per their membership plan.

Accepted Covers:

  • SHA PHC (Primary Health Care)
  • SHA Mwalimu Cover
  • Public Officers Comprehensive Cover

🎓 Mwalimu Comprehensive Cover

Fully accepted and supported. Teachers on the Mwalimu Comprehensive cover receive complete benefits for outpatient services, laboratory testing, ultrasound imaging, and chronic disease management.

Benefits Covered:

  • Medical consultations
  • Diagnostic laboratory testing
  • Ultrasound services
  • Chronic disease management

M-Pesa Mobile Money

Convenient mobile money payments via M-Pesa. Payments can be made before, during, or after service delivery.

Cash Payment

Cash payments are accepted at the facility. Our reception team will guide you through the payment process.

Service Costs: We do not display service charges online to ensure fair negotiated pricing based on individual circumstances. Contact us directly for pricing information or to discuss payment plans.

🎯 Ready for Kenya's Universal Health Coverage (UHC)

We are committed to supporting Kenya's Universal Health Coverage goals and are ready to incorporate other insurance schemes to expand access to quality healthcare for all. Our facility is designed to partner with government and private health programs.
