Scope: code and automated workflow review of the Mwein Next.js platform. This is not clinical acceptance or an independent security assessment.

## Delivered in this increment

- Effective-dated catalogue prices, change reasons, and invoice price-version references.
- Visit cancellation with 11 reasons, including SHA benefit/eligibility outcomes, required explanation and SHA verification reference.
- Permission-controlled cancellation, cancellation history in the clinical timeline, invoice voiding and removal from active queues.
- Blocks for signed consultations, completed orders, confirmed payments, external claims and terminal visit states. SHA-related cancellation is blocked for urgent/emergency visits.
- Closed consultations reject saves from stale screens; cancellation form state resets when switching patients.

## External dependencies

Live SHA eligibility, preauthorisation and claim transmission still require executed contracts, approved facility tariffs, credentials and conformance testing. KHIS submission, automatic messaging and analyser exchange remain preparation-only. Backup/restore, security testing, clinical acceptance and governance evidence must be verified for production operation; passing software tests does not complete these gates.

## SHA interpretation

The cancellation outcome list records staff-reported outcomes; it does not determine benefits or prove a live SHA check. A verification outage does not establish ineligibility. Emergency-care protection follows the official benefits material. Facility-specific coverage must be verified against the applicable executed terms.

## Verification

Prior feature verification: 173 unit tests, 35 migrations, 70 outpatient integration checks, 21 browser tests and production build passed. The stale-screen protection has a separate integration assertion. Deployment status is reported separately after release verification.

Follow-on increment: clinic tariff migration preserves previous 500 KES routine / zero Emergency baselines; administrators edit them under Services & pricing → Consultations, procedures & imaging. Existing invoice values remain unchanged. Urgent/emergency check-in uses zero if a usable tariff is unavailable, preserving access to care. No SHA reimbursement amounts are inferred from these facility prices.
