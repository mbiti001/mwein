## Delivered in this increment

- Effective-dated catalogue prices, change reasons, and invoice price-version references.
- Visit cancellation with 11 reasons, including SHA benefit/eligibility outcomes, required explanation and SHA verification reference.

## External dependencies

Live SHA eligibility, preauthorisation and claim transmission still require executed contracts, approved facility tariffs, credentials and conformance testing. KHIS submission, automatic messaging and analyser exchange remain preparation-only. Backup/restore, security testing, clinical acceptance and governance evidence must be verified for production operation; passing software tests does not complete these gates.

## SHA interpretation

The cancellation outcome list records staff-reported outcomes; it does not determine benefits or prove a live SHA check. A verification outage does not establish ineligibility. Emergency-care protection follows the official benefits material. Facility-specific coverage must be verified against the applicable executed terms.
