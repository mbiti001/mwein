## Explicit limits and next work

- Queue active lists are unbounded; history fetches at most 60 entries from 24 hours, and the screen shows at most 20 per service without a truncation indicator. Supply lists identify truncation but cannot page beyond the latest 100 per kind. Do not describe either as exhaustive history.
- Queue call/start/pause/transfer events retain an actor and infer its facility in `appendAudit`, but omit `sessionId`. Target changes and escalation pass session explicitly. Complete session attribution is a separate improvement; this review does not silently backfill old events.
- Queue escalation records a staff statement and preserves priority. It sends no notification, proves no contact, and implements no accepted clinical handover. Offered/accepted diagnostic responsibility, inactive owners, escalation acceptance and review addenda remain priorities.
- Queue call-next remains enabled when empty/busy until the server rejects the action. Start is a workflow action, not proof of clinician assessment. A transient serialization failure may surface as a generic failure; concurrent-request recovery remains separate work.
- Generic queue print is not a dedicated control-free report. Supply documents use the existing isolated print surface. Physical printer/long-document UAT remains external.
- Malformed stored receipt evidence fails closed with 422 under the shared error handler. Operational data-corruption diagnosis and historical repair are separate from user input validation. Purchase-order descriptions and staff names resolve from current scoped records; goods receipts retain their original snapshot.
- The source inventory reaches zero unclassified routes only for the reviewed exact tree. It is regex-based, detects calls rather than all execution branches, and does not establish session completeness, protected retention, independent security review, legal conformity, DHA certification or clinical approval. Future source changes invalidate fingerprints.
