## Response and incident clocks to preserve

| Obligation | Supplied provision | Engineering treatment / remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| Access: 7 days from request | DPG21 r. 9(4), PDF p. 8 | New intake target enforced. Free access and required explanatory information still need full response evidence. |
| Necessary rectification: 14 days from request | DPG21 r. 10(4), PDF pp. 8–9 | Target enforced; decision and scope remain reviewed. Declined correction has a separate written-reason requirement within 7 days under r. 10(5). |
| Portability: 30 days from request | DPG21 r. 11(3), PDF p. 9 | Target enforced. No fee introduced and no clock suspension for payment/verification. Decline notice within 7 days under r. 11(6) remains a separate gap. |
| Restriction / objection / erasure response: 14 days | DPG21 rr. 7(3), 8(3), 12(3), PDF pp. 7–10 | Not implemented by the current four-type request workflow. Related decisions/notices and exceptions must be modelled. |
| ODPC breach notification | DPA19 s. 43(1)–(3), PDF pp. 29–30 | Controller: without delay, within 72 hours of awareness for the statutory risk-of-harm trigger; delayed notice includes reasons. Processor to controller: without delay and, where reasonably practicable, within 48 hours. These are different duties. |
| DHA CEO health-data breach notice and update | HIM25 r. 11(2), PDF p. 10 | Initial notification within 48 hours of awareness, HMIS 1; corrective measures/mitigation/timelines update within 72 hours **of notification**, not of awareness. Do not substitute the ODPC clock. |

The intake implementation uses fixed elapsed 24-hour days as conservative operational targets, with Nairobi display and no weekend/holiday extension. This is not a legal calendar-calculation opinion. It does not authorize waiting until the target, remove duties to act promptly, or establish that every requested correction must be applied. DISCLOSURE has an explicitly labelled **internal seven-day triage target**, not an invented statutory period. `requestedAt` stores original receipt; `createdAt` retains actual system entry time. Refusal notice and delivery milestones need their own fields in a later batch.
