# Mwein DHA HIE requirements and certification pipeline

Baseline added 16 October 2026 at the owner's request. Public documentation reviewed at [DHA HIE](https://hie-docs.dha.go.ke/); reviewed pages report 28 September 2026 as their last modification. This augments the seven supplied legal documents and existing workflow pipeline. It is an engineering gap register, not certification or authorization to transmit patient data.

## Certification boundary

DHA describes development testing, witnessed UAT, DHA certification and production access as successive stages. We need an approved UAT instance, development credentials, retained demonstration results and the certification decision before national production integration. A Vercel deployment is not HIE acceptance. [Go-live process](https://hie-docs.dha.go.ke/docs/goLive)

The linked [official UAT checklist](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1zFOjatVWf3qmHe1LUPQ-gjwJhIDxpPsdqN-CYSSpU38/edit?usp=sharing) was publicly readable through row 64 of its displayed PHC sheet. The visible checklist covers registry searches, eligibility, consent, duplicate/admission checks, intervention combinations, billing, diagnoses, attachments, preview and submission. Do not claim the whole workbook or other tabs have been reviewed. Obtain the current complete checklist for witnessed UAT. No contact, account creation or third-party message was sent.

## Changes and contradictions to resolve before integration

The [changelog](https://hie-docs.dha.go.ke/changelog) records retirement of the draft clinical FHIR and EMT endpoints, asynchronous SHR bundle acceptance, updated claims request shapes and a controlled OTP fallback for unusable fingerprints. Accepted/queued is not proof that a record was persisted or that a payer approved a claim. Treat fallback eligibility as a server decision, not an operator bypass. Recheck this log and schema versions for each integration batch.

The old [user journey](https://hie-docs.dha.go.ke/docs/userJourney) redirects readers to scenarios and still describes six paths; the [current scenario index](https://hie-docs.dha.go.ke/docs/scenarios/overview) lists eight including two emergency cases. Its general patient-consent wording also coexists with specific emergency doctor-consent treatment. Preserve these distinctions and obtain the approved scenario contract before implementing emergency behavior. Do not infer legal/clinical exceptions from a generic overview.
