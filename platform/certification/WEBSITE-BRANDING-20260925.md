# Website branding in the HMIS

Uses the exact current mweinmedical.co.ke wordmark, verified by live asset hash. The full wordmark is displayed prominently on a white sidebar panel, in the sign-in navigation and on shared document letterheads. Website pulse icon/favicon assets replace the older app mark.

Existing clinical referrals, visit summaries, laboratory/imaging reports, invoices and receipts inherit the shared letterhead. Medicine labels use the wordmark when the visit's facility is Mwein. Other facilities retain their own name without Mwein logo/contact details on documents.

Clinic forms provides printable blank sick sheets, maternity delivery records, goods delivery notes and gate passes with the same letterhead and authorization fields. These are stationery, not electronically issued or saved clinical certificates. Staff must complete them against the source record and authorize them. Populated referrals continue through the existing referral workflow. The print view isolates the selected blank form from other app content and waits for the logo image to decode.

Verification: shared-brand rendering checks, sign-in/mobile checks and browser checks of all four blank forms, with print-media isolation and visual inspection of the HMIS and gate-pass print layout. No new database schema, patient data or clinical policy is introduced.
