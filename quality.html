Our SafeCare Commitment

Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI)

As a Level 2 Primary Healthcare facility, Mwein Medical Services is dedicated to the international SafeCare standards, ensuring patient safety, structural quality, and clinical excellence.

1. Clinical Protocols & Safety

All medical interventions are guided by the Kenya National Clinical Guidelines. We strictly enforce Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) measures, ensuring a sterilized and safe environment for every patient.

2. Qualified Healthcare Workforce

Our facility is staffed exclusively by professionals licensed by KMPDC and PPB. We conduct routine Continuous Medical Education (CME) and competency assessments to maintain high service standards.

3. Diagnostic Accuracy

Our laboratory and ultrasound equipment undergo regular calibration, maintenance, and daily Quality Control (QC) runs to guarantee precise, reliable test results for effective treatment.

4. Patient-Centered Care

Quality healthcare goes beyond medical treatment. We prioritize respectful communication, transparent billing, and active patient feedback to continuously improve our services.
